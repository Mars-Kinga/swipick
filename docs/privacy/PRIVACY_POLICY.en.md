# Swipick Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 13, 2026**

Swipick is a photo and video review app. This policy explains what the app reads, what it keeps on your device, and when information may leave your device. The publisher of Swipick is **[DEVELOPER NAME]**. For privacy questions, contact **[PUBLIC SUPPORT EMAIL]**.

## Photos access and processing

Swipick requests access to your Photos library so you can review photos and videos, compare suggestions, organize items, and make changes you choose. You can grant full or limited access through iOS. The app uses Apple's PhotoKit and Vision frameworks to examine the photos you allow it to access. Its suggestion analysis, including image comparison and screenshot text recognition, runs on your device. Suggestions are advisory; the app does not automatically delete photos or videos.

We do not collect data from Swipick through the app. The app does not upload your photo or video content, recognized screenshot text, or on-device analysis results to a server operated by us. It does not use an advertising or third-party analytics SDK, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

## Information stored on your device

To make review sessions resumable, Swipick stores review decisions, pending changes, settings, counts, and limited information about the photos involved, such as Photos library identifiers and dates. It also stores a regenerable analysis cache that can contain image feature data, similarity and quality scores, resource hashes, and screenshot classifications. Recognized screenshot text is used to classify a screenshot and is not saved as a transcript in the analysis cache. The app may temporarily create a local copy of a media file when you share it or convert a Live Photo.

This information stays in the app's local storage unless you choose to share media or your device's own backup settings include app data. It is used to provide the app's features and is not used for advertising.

## iCloud Photos, sharing, and library changes

If you use iCloud Photos, Apple's Photos service may download a preview or original media file when you view, play, share, inspect, or convert an item. Automatic download of full-quality photos while browsing is off by default and can be changed in Swipick's settings. Suggestion scans do not request iCloud originals. iCloud Photos and device backups are Apple services subject to your Apple settings and Apple's privacy practices; Swipick does not operate those services.

When you tap Share, Swipick prepares the selected item and opens the iOS share sheet. The destination you choose may receive that item under its own privacy terms. Swipick does not select a sharing destination for you.

Deleting, favoriting, or assigning items to an existing album is applied to the Photos library through PhotoKit after you confirm the relevant pending actions. Creating a new album or converting a Live Photo can update the Photos library when you choose that action. A converted still is checked before the original is queued for deletion. If you use iCloud Photos or a shared library, changes to Photos may sync to other devices or participants. Apple's Recently Deleted behavior and recovery period are controlled by Photos, not Swipick.

## Your choices and deletion

You can limit or revoke Photos access in iOS Settings at any time. Revoking access stops further access to those photos; it does not by itself remove review records already saved in Swipick's local storage. In Swipick's settings, you can pause automatic suggestion analysis and clear the saved suggestion analysis data. This clear action does not erase your review decisions or pending changes. You can change or undo review decisions in the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data from the device, subject to any backups managed by iOS.

Swipick has no account or developer-operated cloud storage, so there is no account data held on our servers to request for deletion. For questions about this policy, use the contact address above.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be updated, and the current policy will be available from the app's privacy policy link and its App Store listing.
