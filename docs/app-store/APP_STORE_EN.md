# Swipick — English App Store copy

## App name

Swipick

## Subtitle

Review photos with confidence

## Promotional text

Go through photos and videos one at a time. Get on-device suggestions for duplicates, similar shots, and temporary screenshots. You make every keep-or-delete decision.

## Description

Make space for the photos you care about, one choice at a time.

Swipick gives you a focused way to review your photo library. Swipe left to add a photo or video to a deletion list, swipe right to keep it, or set it aside for later. Undo a decision, review by month, year, media type, or album, and pick up where you left off.

Find photos worth a closer look:

• On-device suggestions bring together exact duplicates, visually similar photos, and older screenshots that may contain temporary information.
• Compare similar shots side by side. Swipick can suggest which to keep using on-device image analysis, but different versions can all stay in your library.
• An exact duplicate is identified only after the underlying photo resources match. Suggestions never delete anything automatically.

Stay in control of changes. Review the deletion list before applying it to Photos. You can also mark favorites, organize photos into albums, preview videos and Live Photos, and convert a Live Photo into a still image. When you convert a Live Photo, Swipick checks the new still before placing the original on the deletion list.

Your review history and suggestion analysis are stored on your device. Swipick has no account, ads, or tracking. If you use iCloud Photos, the app starts with available previews; you can choose whether to download the photo you are viewing in full quality.

Requires iOS 26 or later and access to the photos you choose to review.
